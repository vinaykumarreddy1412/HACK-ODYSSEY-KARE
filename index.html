01 / ABOUT THE ODYSSEY

BUILD BEYOND
THE ORDINARY.

Hack Odyssey 4.0 is a premier 24-hour national level hackathon conducted by Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education under the Euphoria 2026 event. It brings students together to explore artificial intelligence and emerging technologies, solve real-world problems, collaborate with peers and build innovative solutions with meaningful impact.

Artificial Intelligence Emerging Tech Collaboration Problem Solving Sustainability

AI & Emerging Tech Focus

Leverage cutting-edge LLMs, computer vision, generative AI, and embedded IoT architectures to solve critical challenges.

Real-World SDG Impact

Direct alignment with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals ensuring real, tangible impact.

National Stage & Mentorship

Network with veteran researchers, industry leaders, and tech pioneers guiding your implementation.
